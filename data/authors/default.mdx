## Why this journal

A _ḥāshiya_ is a marginal gloss, the notes a scholar writes beside a text to explain it. This journal is a gloss in that sense: long-form writing on God, scripture and the traditions that read them, and careful answers to questions about Islam.

It is written by a Muslim, openly, for two kinds of reader at once: Muslims who want depth, and people of other faiths or none who want to understand. Nothing here assumes you already know the terms. Arabic words are explained where they appear and collected in the [glossary](/glossary).

## Sources and translations

Primary texts are always quoted in full, with their reference and translator, and set apart from commentary so you can tell the source from the gloss.

- **Qur'an.** Arabic text with an English translation, usually Sahih International unless another is named beside the verse.
- **Ḥadīth.** Quoted from the major collections with collection and number. Grades are those given by the collection or by the scholar named in the text.
- **Bible and other scripture.** Quoted from the translation named beside each passage, most often the ESV.
- **Transliteration.** A simplified academic system, described in [a note on transliteration](/blog/sample-transliteration).

Every post links to its source file. If you find an error in a quotation or a reference, use **Suggest a correction** at the end of the post.
