## Where religions contradict

Some differences between religions are differences of practice. Others are claims about the same facts that cannot all be true. Christianity teaches that Jesus is the Son of God; Islam teaches that God has no son. Whatever one thinks of either, both cannot be right. Saying all religions are equally true sounds generous, but it quietly says that each is wrong about what it considers most important.

## Truth held in common

Islam does not say other religions are false in everything. It teaches that every prophet brought the same message of <Term translit="tawḥīd">God's oneness</Term>, and that every person is born with a <Term translit="fiṭra">disposition</Term> to recognise it:

<Hadith
  collection="Sahih al-Bukhari"
  number="1385"
  grade="Sahih"
  narrator="Narrated by Abū Hurayra"
  arabic="كُلُّ مَوْلُودٍ يُولَدُ عَلَى الْفِطْرَةِ، فَأَبَوَاهُ يُهَوِّدَانِهِ أَوْ يُنَصِّرَانِهِ أَوْ يُمَجِّسَانِهِ"
>
  Every child is born upon the fiṭra; then his parents make him a Jew, a Christian or a Magian.
</Hadith>

So it is no surprise to find the oneness of God still stated in earlier scripture:

  originalLang="he"
>
  Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one.
</Scripture>

## Why Islam alone

The claim is not that Muslims are better people. It is that the message itself reached them unaltered, while in other traditions it was mixed with additions or lost in part. The Qur'an states the claim plainly:

<Quran
  ref="3:85"
  arabic="وَمَن يَبْتَغِ غَيْرَ الْإِسْلَامِ دِينًا فَلَن يُقْبَلَ مِنْهُ وَهُوَ فِي الْآخِرَةِ مِنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ"
  translation="And whoever desires other than Islam as religion – never will it be accepted from him, and he, in the Hereafter, will be among the losers."
  translator="Sahih International"
/>

A claim like this needs evidence, and the rest of Part III of the course sets it out: the proofs of prophethood, the Qur'an's challenge and its preservation.
