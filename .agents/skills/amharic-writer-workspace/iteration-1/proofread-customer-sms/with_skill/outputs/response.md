Here's the corrected SMS. It's also saved to `sms-fixed.txt`.

```
ውድ ደንበኛ፣ ትዕዛዝዎን በተሳካ ሁኔታ ተቀብለናል። እባክዎ ክፍያዎን እስከ ነገ ድረስ ይፈጽሙ። ከወሰኑ በኋላ ያግኙን። ስለ ትዕግስትዎ እናመሰግናለን።
```

**What I changed**

1. **ትዕዛዝህ → ትዕዛዝዎ(ን).** The draft used the familiar "you" for a man (‑ህ) here and the polite form (እባክዎ, ክፍያዎን, ይፈጽሙ) everywhere else. Customer messages should use the polite form throughout, and it doesn't assume the customer is a man.
2. **ትዕዛዝህ … ተቀብሏል → ትዕዛዝዎን … ተቀብለናል.** As written, ተቀብሏል says the order received something. Putting it in the active voice ("we have received your order") fixes this.
3. **ውሳኔ ከወሰዱ በኋላ → ከወሰኑ በኋላ.** "ውሳኔ መውሰድ" copies the English "take a decision" word for word. In Amharic the plain verb መወሰን means "to decide".
4. **እኛን ያግኙን → ያግኙን.** The ‑ን at the end of ያግኙን already means "us", so እኛን said it twice.
5. **Punctuation.** I replaced `::` with the Ethiopic full stop `።`, removed the spaces before `፣` and `።`, and added the missing `።` at the end.

I left your wording otherwise as it was. ትዕግስት is a valid spelling (ትዕግሥት is the other one), so I didn't touch it.

**Worth checking before you send**

- **"ከወሰኑ በኋላ ያግኙን" (contact us after you decide).** It isn't clear what the customer is deciding. They have already ordered and are being asked to pay. If you mean "contact us if you have questions", say **ጥያቄ ካለዎት ያግኙን።** Or drop the sentence.
- **"ስለ ትዕግስትዎ እናመሰግናለን" (thank you for your patience)** usually follows a delay. If nothing has kept the customer waiting, **ስለመረጡን እናመሰግናለን።** ("thank you for choosing us") may fit better.
- **"እስከ ነገ" (by tomorrow)** depends on when the SMS goes out. If the deadline is a fixed date or time, give it in the Ethiopian calendar, and for a time say which clock (for example ከቀኑ 11 ሰዓት = 5:00 p.m.).
- **Length.** The message is 95 characters. An SMS in Ethiopic script holds 70 characters, so this one goes out in 2 parts. You could save space by shortening or removing the decision sentence.
- Before this goes to customers, have a native Amharic speaker on your team read it once more.
