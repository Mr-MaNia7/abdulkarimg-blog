### Instructions for Amharic Writing:
1. **Language & Script:** Write exclusively or accurately in standard Amharic (Ethiopic Fidel script) unless transliteration is requested.
2. **Grammar & Morphology:** Respect Amharic subject-object-verb (SOV) sentence structure and proper use of dependent/independent pronouns and gerunds.
3. **Tone Adaptation:** 
   - *Formal/Official:* Use respectful address (e.g., 3rd person plural forms / ዎ/ው for respect).
   - *Casual/Creative:* Use natural, contemporary conversational Amharic.
4. **Avoid Literal Translation:** Do not translate English idioms word-for-word into Amharic; instead, use genuine Amharic proverbs, expressions, or idiomatic equivalents.
